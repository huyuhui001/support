Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Last updated: August 2025 | 最后更新：2025年8月

📋 Overview | 概述

English: This privacy policy describes how we handle information when you use our applications and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

中文： 本隐私政策描述了当您使用我们的应用程序和服务时，我们如何处理信息。我们致力于保护您的隐私，并对我们的数据实践保持透明。

✅ We respect your privacy | 我们尊重您的隐私

✅ We protect your data | 我们保护您的数据

✅ We are transparent | 我们保持透明

✅ We follow best practices | 我们遵循最佳实践

🔒 Data Collection | 数据收集

English: Our applications are designed with privacy in mind. We minimize data collection and only gather information that is necessary to provide and improve our services. Depending on the specific application, this may include:

  • App usage statistics (anonymous)
  • Crash reports (anonymous)
  • User preferences and settings (stored locally)

中文： 我们的应用程序在设计时充分考虑了隐私。我们最小化数据收集，仅收集提供和改进服务所必需的信息。根据具体应用，这可能包括：

  • 应用使用统计（匿名）
  • 崩溃报告（匿名）
  • 用户偏好和设置（本地存储）

💾 Data Storage | 数据存储

English: User data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. Depending on the application, data may be stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • In encrypted cloud storage (if sync features are enabled)
  • On secure servers with appropriate safeguards

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

中文： 用户数据使用行业标准实践安全存储。根据应用程序的不同，数据可能存储在：

  • 您的设备本地
  • 加密云存储中（如果启用同步功能）
  • 具有适当安全措施的安全服务器上

我们实施适当的技术和组织措施，保护您的数据免遭未经授权的访问、更改或破坏。

🌐 Third-Party Services | 第三方服务

English: Some of our applications may integrate with third-party services to enhance functionality. These may include:

  • Analytics services (to improve app performance)
  • Cloud storage services (for data synchronization)
  • Payment processors (for in-app purchases)

Any third-party services we use are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

中文： 我们的某些应用程序可能会集成第三方服务以增强功能。这些可能包括：

  • 分析服务（用于改进应用性能）
  • 云存储服务（用于数据同步）
  • 支付处理器（用于应用内购买）

我们使用的任何第三方服务都必须维护您数据的机密性和安全性。

👤 User Rights | 用户权利

English: You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us through the channels provided below.

中文： 您有权：

  • 访问您的个人数据
  • 要求更正不准确的数据
  • 要求删除您的数据
  • 撤回对数据处理的同意
  • 导出您的数据

要行使这些权利，请通过下方提供的渠道联系我们。

👶 Children's Privacy | 儿童隐私

English: We take children's privacy seriously. Our applications designed for children comply with applicable children's privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent where required by law.

中文： 我们非常重视儿童的隐私。我们为儿童设计的应用程序遵守适用的儿童隐私法律。在法律要求的情况下，我们不会在未获得适当家长同意的情况下故意收集儿童的个人信息。

🔄 Changes to This Policy | 政策变更

English: We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

中文： 我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策，以反映我们实践的变化或出于其他运营、法律或监管原因。任何更新都将在此页面上发布，并更新"最后更新"日期。我们鼓励您定期查看本政策。

📧 Contact Us | 联系我们

English: If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through:

  • GitHub Issues: https://github.com/huyuhui001/support/issues
  • GitHub Discussions: https://github.com/huyuhui001/support/discussions

中文： 如果您对本隐私政策有任何问题、疑虑或建议，请通过以下方式联系我们：

  • GitHub Issues: https://github.com/huyuhui001/support/issues
  • GitHub Discussions: https://github.com/huyuhui001/support/discussions

✅ Summary | 总结

English: In summary, we are committed to:

  • ✅ Protecting your privacy and data
  • ✅ Being transparent about our practices
  • ✅ Following industry best practices
  • ✅ Complying with applicable privacy laws
  • ✅ Responding to your concerns promptly

中文： 总之，我们致力于：

  • ✅ 保护您的隐私和数据
  • ✅ 对我们的实践保持透明
  • ✅ 遵循行业最佳实践
  • ✅ 遵守适用的隐私法律
  • ✅ 及时回应您的关切
